Our Programs

Martial arts
for everyone

Programs specially designed for individuals with autism spectrum disorder or physical limitations.

Flagship program

KaraTED

An adapted karate program specifically developed for individuals with autism spectrum disorder. A structured, visual approach that promotes learning basic techniques while developing motor skills, concentration, and self-confidence.

  • Adapted karate techniques
  • Visual and structured approach
  • Small group ratios
  • Scholarships for low-income families
Adapted Jiu-Jitsu

BJJTED

An adapted Brazilian jiu-jitsu program focused on ground work, proprioception, and physical contact management. Ideal for developing body awareness and social skills in a controlled environment.

  • Adapted Brazilian jiu-jitsu
  • Proprioception development
  • Physical contact management
  • Specialized supervision
Adapted Judo

Partnership with Judo Québec

ADAMA collaborates with Judo Québec to make judo accessible to people with special needs across the province.

Club training

Supporting judo clubs in integrating practitioners with special needs.

Regular sessions

Weekly classes at multiple centers across Québec.

Adapted competitions

Participation in Canadian championships with adapted categories.

Scholarships

Scholarship program

Funds raised provide scholarships for youth from low-income families to participate in adapted activities.

Every contribution allows a young person to discover the benefits of adapted martial arts.
