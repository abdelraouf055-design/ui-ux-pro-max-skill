Training

Become a certified
instructor

Training in adapted martial arts teaching techniques for individuals with ASD or physical limitations.

Who it's for

Who is this training for

Our training is designed for anyone who wants to teach martial arts inclusively.

Instructors

Martial arts instructors looking to adapt their teaching.

Companions

Companions and specialized educators working with this clientele.

Parents

Parents who want to understand the tools to support their children.

Professionals

Healthcare professionals and workers supporting individuals with special needs.

Curriculum

What you'll learn

Understanding ASD

Gain a deep understanding of autism spectrum disorder and its impact on motor learning.

Teaching methods

Master clear, structured teaching tools for effective and inclusive instruction.

Adapted techniques

Learn to adapt martial arts techniques to each practitioner's abilities and needs.

Group management

Strategies for managing mixed groups of neurotypical and neuroatypical practitioners.

Communication

Visual, verbal, and gestural communication techniques adapted to different profiles.

Assessment

Assessment and progress tracking tools adapted to individual goals.
