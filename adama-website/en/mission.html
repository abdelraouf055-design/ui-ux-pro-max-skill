Our Mission

Martial arts as a tool
for inclusion

ADAMA promotes the integration of young people with autism into adapted martial arts recreational and sporting activities.

Our story

Over 15 years of commitment

ADAMA was born from the belief that martial arts can transform the lives of people with special needs. Our unique approach combines the rigor of traditional martial arts with adapted teaching methods.

We develop and deliver a clear, structured teaching method that enables instructors, educators, and parents to achieve success with this clientele.

Foundation

ADAMA is created

Launch of the association in Montreal with a vision of inclusion through martial arts.

Growth

KaraTED & BJJTED programs

Development of specialized adapted karate and Brazilian jiu-jitsu programs.

Partnerships

Judo Québec

Partnership with Judo Québec for the development of adapted judo across the province.

Today

National reach

Participation in Canadian championships and training instructors across the country.
